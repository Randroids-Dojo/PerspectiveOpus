Perspective Opus

Privacy policy, effective 8 October 2026

Perspective Opus is a game by toyboxes.games for Android and the web. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (which movements you have played, the notes you have found, your best times) and your settings (volume, motion, detail) are saved only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data removes them.

What we do not do

No accounts, no sign in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone. The Android app does not use the internet.

The web version

The web version is served by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. The game itself sends nothing back.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
